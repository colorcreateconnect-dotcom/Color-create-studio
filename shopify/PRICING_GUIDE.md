# Color Create Studio — Pricing Strategy

> Read this whenever you feel the urge to lowball. Your work is **custom, one-of-one,
> meaning-built design** — not clip-art on a template. Price it like it.

## The core rule: price on VALUE, not time
Never price by "how long it took." Price by what it's *worth to the buyer*: a memorial
they'll keep forever, a baby's first milestone, a survivor's celebration. That symbolism
(the three B's for the family they're creating, the hearts, the story you build into every
piece) **is** the product. It is premium by nature.

## The ladder (why it's built this way)

| Tier | Price range | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **On-ramp** | $5–$15 | Get first-time buyers in the door cheaply. Not where you make money. | Digital downloads, single printables, single Canva templates, small add-ons |
| **Custom design** | $16–$70 | Your bread and butter — bespoke, built-from-blank-page design. | Custom invitations, shirts, favor labels, business graphics |
| **Premium design** | $70–$165 | Bigger custom pieces & licensing. | Vinyl/DTF files, brand mini-packs, single book pages, consultations, covers |
| **Suites & bundles** | $95–$295 | Coordinated multi-piece sets. The "do it all for me" offer. | Celebration suite, memorial suite, event/party bundles, business starter |
| **High-end** | $300–$650 | Anchors. Full done-for-you book creation. | Custom story writing/illustration packages, full custom books |

## Why the high anchor matters (this cures lowballing)
When a **$650** illustration package and a **$295** celebration suite sit on your menu,
your **$45 custom shirt** and **$65 consultation** feel like an *easy yes*. The premium
tier reframes everything below it as affordable. Remove the anchor and your mid-tier
suddenly looks "expensive." Keep it.

## Don't scare people away — but don't apologize either
- The **on-ramp tier** is your "no-risk, try-me" entry. It keeps you approachable.
- The **custom tiers** are firm. If someone wants bespoke work, that's premium — full stop.
- Use **add-ons** (rush, extra revision, commercial license, ownership transfer) to raise
  order value *without* raising your base price. People who want more, pay more.

## Levers you can pull instead of discounting
1. **Add-ons & upgrades** — premium cardstock, lamination, gold binding, gift packaging.
2. **Bundles** — package matching pieces so the *bundle* is the deal, not a price cut.
3. **Tiered delivery** — Digital → Print-Ready → Print + Ship lets budget buyers self-select
   without you dropping your design fee.

## Editing prices
All prices live in `generate_products_csv.py`. Change the numbers there and re-run
`python3 generate_products_csv.py` to rebuild `products_import.csv`. Everything imports as
**Draft / unpublished**, so you always review before anything goes live.
