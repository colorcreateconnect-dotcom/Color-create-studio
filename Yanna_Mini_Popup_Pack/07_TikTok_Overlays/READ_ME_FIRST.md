# Where Did the Overlays Go?

The CapCut-ready overlays ARE the transparent PNGs in
06_Transparent_PNGs. Drag those straight into CapCut.

This pack previously duplicated every asset at 800px, but the
upscaled copies contained no extra detail and quintupled the pack
size. CapCut scales overlays automatically - a native-resolution
transparent PNG at 30 percent screen height looks identical to a
pre-upscaled one.

Workflow: import from 06_Transparent_PNGs, place, scale, animate.
