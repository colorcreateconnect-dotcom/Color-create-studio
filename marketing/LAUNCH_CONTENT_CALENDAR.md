# Launch Content Calendar — Color Create Studio

A **14-day launch runway** to announce the store across **Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook**,
for both the **Company brand** (@colorcreatestudio) and your **Personal brand**.

**Strategy:** Personal brand = face, story, behind-the-scenes → builds trust and funnels
attention to the company. Company brand = products, offers, proof → converts.

**Content pillars:** 🎬 Behind-the-Scenes · 📚 Educational · 💡 Story/Personal · 🛍️ Sales/Offer · 🌿 Lifestyle

**Cadence:** 1 main post/day per account + 1–2 stories/day. Reels/TikTok > static for reach.
Every post = **Hook (first 1–3 sec) → Value/Story → CTA**.

> Photographer's-eye + VAK notes are included per post. V = Visual, A = Auditory, K = Kinesthetic.

## Repeatable weekly rhythm (after launch)
- **Mon** — Motivation/Story (Personal) + New offer (Company)
- **Tue** — Educational/Tutorial
- **Wed** — Behind-the-scenes build/time-lapse
- **Thu** — Product spotlight + CTA
- **Fri** — Lifestyle / day-in-the-life
- **Sat** — Social proof / UGC / reviews
- **Sun** — Engagement (poll, Q&A, "design this with me")

## Hook bank (steal these)
- "POV: your [event] but every detail matches."
- "Stop overpaying for [cheap alternative]. Here's the difference."
- "Watch a blank page become a [product]."
- "3 things your [invite/shirt/design] is secretly saying."
- "I made this for a customer and cried a little. Here's the story."
- "The $[X] mistake people make when ordering custom design."

## Caption formula
1. **Hook line** (curiosity or bold claim)
2. **Story or value** (2–4 lines, VAK words)
3. **CTA** (one clear action)
4. **Hashtags** (5–10: mix niche + local, e.g. #customshirts #partydesign #[yourcity]smallbusiness)

## Notes
- Reels/TikToks drive new reach; carousels drive saves; stories drive trust + DMs.
- Reuse one shoot 3 ways: Reel + carousel stills + story clips.
- Tie every product post back to a **published collection link** (see HOMEPAGE_WIRING_GUIDE).
- This is where **PRISM** plugs in later — auto-drafting posts/hooks/shot lists for your approval.
