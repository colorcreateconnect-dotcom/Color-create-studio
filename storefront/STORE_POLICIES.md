## 1. REFUND & RETURN POLICY

**Because nearly everything we make is custom, personalized, or digital, most orders are
non-refundable. Please read before you order.**

### Custom & personalized items
All custom and personalized designs (shirts, invitations, party suites, memorials, etc.) are
**made to order specifically for you and cannot be returned, refunded, or exchanged** once the
design process has begun. By ordering, you acknowledge the item is personalized.

### Digital products & downloads
Digital files, templates, and downloads are **final sale and non-refundable** once the file has
been delivered or accessed, due to the nature of digital goods.

### Proof approval = your responsibility
For custom orders, you'll receive a **digital proof to approve before anything is printed or
finalized.** Please check spelling, names, dates, and details carefully. **Once you approve a
proof, you accept responsibility for any errors it contains** — reprints due to approved
mistakes are not free. If *we* make an error versus your approved proof, we fix it at no cost.

### Damaged, defective, or wrong item
If a **physical** item arrives damaged, defective, or different from your approved proof,
contact us within **[3 days]** of delivery with photos and we'll **replace or reprint it** at no
charge. We don't offer cash refunds on custom work, but we'll make it right.

### Cancellations
Orders can be cancelled for a refund **only before design work begins** (typically within
**[24 hours]** of ordering). Once we've started creating, the order is non-cancellable.

### How to reach us
Email **colorcreateconnect@gmail.com** with your order number. We respond within **[1–2 business days]**.

## 2. SHIPPING POLICY

### Processing & turnaround
- **Custom design proofs:** first proof within **[1–3 business days]** of receiving your details.
- **Digital / template orders:** delivered within **[24 hours]** (often same day) after purchase
  (and after proof approval for custom digital files).
- **Print + ship orders:** produced within **[3–5 business days]** after proof approval, then shipped.

> Turnaround starts once we have everything we need (names, photos, colors, dates) and your
> proof is approved — not at time of purchase. Need it fast? Add the **Rush** option.

### Shipping methods, costs & areas
- Rates are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination.
- We currently ship within **[your regions, e.g. the United States]**.
- You'll receive **tracking** by email once your order ships.
- **Local pickup** is available where offered at checkout (no shipping charge).

### Delays, lost & stolen packages
Once an order ships, delivery timelines are in the carrier's hands. We're not responsible for
carrier delays, or lost/stolen packages once marked delivered — but we'll help you file a claim
and do what we can to make it right.

### Digital items
Digital and Canva-template orders are delivered **by email/download — no shipping required.**
