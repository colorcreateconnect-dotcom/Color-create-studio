# Launch 40 — Publish These First

You don't need all 303 live to launch. Image + **Set Active** these ~40 hero products
(3–5 per collection) and your store looks full and finished. Roll out the rest over the week.

**How to use:** in Products, search each handle (or title), add image(s) + alt text from
`seo_reference.csv`, then bulk-select these and **Actions → Set as Active**.

> Pick order: do the **anchors/packages first** (they make the store look premium), then the
> popular individual items, then the low-ticket entry products.
