## VERSION A — Full origin story (recommended)

### Made by hand. Built from stories.

Every order here starts the same way it always has: with a blank page.

I'm **Yanna Camile**, the hands behind Color Create Studio. I don't pull designs off a
shelf. I throw elements onto an empty canvas — colors, shapes, names, little symbols — and
then I move them, recolor them, reshape them, erase them, and build them back up until the
piece says something *true* about the person it's for.

That's the part most people never see. On one baby shower design, there were three little
B's and a cluster of hearts — not decoration, but a quiet picture of the family being
created. That's how I think about every shirt, invitation, keepsake, and book: **what's the
story underneath, and how do we make people feel it?**
