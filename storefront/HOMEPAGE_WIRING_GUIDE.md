# Homepage Wiring Guide — Color Create Studio

A slot-by-slot plan for connecting your **16 collections** to the homepage in the
**Theme Editor** (Online Store → Themes → Customize). This kills the "$0 / Connect a
collection" placeholders and merchandises the store so the right things sell first.

> **Before this works:** products must be **Active** (published). Draft products won't
> show even once a section is connected. See `GO_LIVE_CHECKLIST.md`.

## Exact steps per section

1. **Online Store → Themes → Customize.**
2. Click a section (e.g. a "Featured collection" block) in the left panel.
3. In its settings, find **Collection** → **Select collection** → choose the one from the table.
4. Set **products to show** (e.g. 6–8) and toggle "View all" on.
5. Edit the **heading/subheading** to the copy above (or your own).
6. **Save** (top right). Repeat for each section.

### To add or reorder sections
- Use **Add section** at the bottom of the page to insert "Featured collection,"
  "Featured products," "Image with text," "Rich text," etc.
- Drag sections in the left panel to reorder.

### For the three-division block (section #3)
Use an **Image-with-text** or **Multicolumn** section with 3 columns:
- **Custom Design Studio** → links to `/collections/custom-celebration-design` (or a "Shop Custom" page)
- **Nova Lai Story World** → links to `/collections/nova-lai-kids-collection`
- **Trap N' Chill Zone** → links to `/collections/trap-n-chill-adult-collection`
Add a strong image for each (your brand art / mockups).

## Merchandising tips
- **Lead with custom celebration** — it's your highest-margin, highest-intent offer.
- **Put Bundles + Templates mid-page** — bundles raise order value; templates catch the
  budget/DIY buyer who'd otherwise bounce.
- **Keep Trap N' Chill clearly 21+/adult-labeled** and lower on the page so the first
  impression stays family-friendly for celebration shoppers.
- **Hide empty sections** (social feed with no posts) rather than show placeholders — an
  empty section reads as "unfinished," a hidden one reads as "clean."
- Once you have sales data, swap section #4 to a real **"Bestsellers"** automated collection.
