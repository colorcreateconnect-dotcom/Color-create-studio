# Collection Cover Recipe — Color Create Studio

A batch recipe to make all **16 collection covers** consistent in Canva (~20–30 min).
**One master template, swap 3 things per cover:** (1) title text, (2) color, (3) one icon or photo.

> I can't render the images for you, but follow this and they'll look like a cohesive,
> professional set. Use your **brand kit** fonts/logo throughout.

---

## Master template (build once, duplicate 16×)
- **Size:** 1200 × 1200 px (square) — or match your theme's collection-image ratio.
- **Layout:**
  - Full-bleed **color background** (or a color block over a subtle photo at ~30% opacity)
  - **Collection title** centered or lower-third, in your brand display font, large + bold
  - Small **Color Create Studio logo** or wordmark, corner
  - Optional: one **icon or product photo** that signals the category
- **Consistency rules:** same font, same title position, same logo placement on all 16.
  Only the **color + title + icon** change. That's what makes it look like a set.

## Fast workflow in Canva
1. Build the **master** once with your fonts/logo placed.
2. **Duplicate** the page 16 times.
3. On each: change the **background color** (per table), the **title text**, and swap the **icon/photo**.
4. Use Canva's **brand kit** so colors/fonts stay locked.
5. **Download all** as PNG → upload to each collection in Shopify (Collections → click → add image).

## Tips
- If you have a strong real **product photo** for a category, use it *behind* a color overlay so
  the title stays readable and the set still feels unified.
- Keep titles **short** (1–3 words) — they're tiny on mobile.
- Test on your phone: collection tiles should be legible at thumbnail size.
- When you're ready, **PRISM's Creative skill** can auto-generate on-brand covers like these for
  approval — this recipe is the brief it would follow.
